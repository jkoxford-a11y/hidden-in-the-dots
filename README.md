# Hidden in the Dots

Four hands-on Gestalt demos for a phone, ending on "the whole is greater than the sum of the parts":

1. Common fate: press and hold, and random dots become a dog
2. Closure: tap, and a triangle appears that nobody drew
3. Proximity vs. similarity: drag the slider, and columns become rows
4. Figure-ground: tap, and the vase becomes two faces

Live: https://jkoxford-a11y.github.io/hidden-in-the-dots/

In the dog demo, every dot moves at the same speed and lives for under half a second, so a still frame shows nothing. While you hold, the dots inside the dog's outline share one direction; all others wander. Add `?debug` to the URL to color the dog's dots; `?s=2` opens screen 3.

Built for the ACT 2026 symposium "Blueprint First" (Jon K. Oxford & Darah Oxford).
